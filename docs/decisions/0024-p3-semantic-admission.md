# ADR-0024: P3 — semantic admission, replay, packs (designs 16–18, consolidated)
- **Status**: accepted · 2026-08-20 (all three designs critique-PASS) · **Amended 2026-09-14 (Amendment 1)**: the human approved design 16's first slice, on its Q1–Q13 as recommended.
- **EvalSuite (16)**: eval gate runs once pre-canary (canary progression is SLO-judged — scope pinned); datasets and reports are content-addressed artifacts binding (dataset, judge, revision) digests — rerun-shopping structurally impossible; **fail-closed everywhere** (empty dataset, judge down, budget out ⇒ no pass). **Per-run eval identity**: the eval Job holds its own SVID; the compiler grants it candidate-route admission temporarily (launch→end) — SVIDs are never lent; runner images (third-party, pack-delivered) reach exactly {candidate, pinned KG, judge egress}. EvalRunner is a shipped provider slot (`evalrunner/v1`, DeepEval first, Inspect second) with cross-runner parity on mechanical metrics; `tool_correctness` v1 = target-sequence (capture-independent); eval-principal posture pinned (capture full, KG scope mirrors target); cost baseline = trailing 7d median.
- **Replay (17)**: sessions are views over the receipt stream, never copies; re-drive requires `capture: full` on the initial hop (bodies exist at full); **mocked replay freezes the whole world — LLM hops mocked by default** (`--live-llm` = the drift-probing variant, determinism claim dropped); the mock runs in-cluster (Job + Service, compiled fail-closed route); divergence is a finding, not an error; replays are receipted under synthetic principals; sampler over-samples failures and counts exclusions.
- **Packs (18)**: `pack/v1` — closed facet catalog (new kinds = contract revision); cluster-scoped Pack CR, RBAC + source-allowlist trust root, provenance displayed; **multi-source filter composition by priority bands** (platform/compliance outrank packs by construction; same-field = hard conflict; byte-deterministic merge — ADR-0028 note); pack filters route through the compiler (no gateway-config backdoor); digest-referenced content with a content-addressed KV cache for small text facets; upgrade split (behavioral facets propagate w/ rollback; authored patterns never auto-propagate); 09/19 handover via `pack adopt` around unchanged artifacts.
- **Consequences**: the flagship demo (`HELD → eval → canary → 100%`) is fully specified end-to-end; the contracts ledger now carries every socket contract; remaining backlog = P4 (20–24), P5 (25), ent (26–27).

## Amendment 1 (2026-09-14, the human's approval of design 16's first slice)

**The human approved design 16's first slice for implementation on 2026-09-14.** Approval came after twelve critiques of that slice. The eighth returned PASS (`docs/designs/reviews/16-a10-critique.md`: 0 BLOCKER, 0 MAJOR, 6 MINOR), and design 16 A11 closed its minors. The ninth (`docs/designs/reviews/16-a11-critique.md`: FAIL, 0 BLOCKER, 2 MAJOR, 6 MINOR) is answered by design 16 A12, the tenth (`docs/designs/reviews/16-a12-critique.md`: FAIL, 0 BLOCKER, 2 MAJOR, 6 MINOR) by A13, and the eleventh (`docs/designs/reviews/16-a13-critique.md`: FAIL, 0 BLOCKER, 2 MAJOR, 8 MINOR) by A15. **The twelfth returned PASS** (`docs/designs/reviews/16-a15-critique.md`: 0 BLOCKER, 0 MAJOR, 7 MINOR), closing both of the eleventh's MAJORs against the code and finding the slice ready; A17 closes its minors. None of these amendments changes a decision among Q1 to Q13. Only §1.1 of design 16 (`docs/designs/16-evalsuite.md`) is approved. The rest of design 16 is not, and must not be implemented. The human answered design 16's Q1 to Q13 each as recommended. §1.1's "Decisions of the human" lists them.

**What the first slice changes in this ADR.** For the first slice, and only for it, these statements of the EvalSuite (16) bullet above do not hold:

- **"eval gate runs once pre-canary (canary progression is SLO-judged)".** The slice has no canary. A revision is evaluated before it takes any traffic, then promoted wholesale or held: blue/green only.
- **"rerun-shopping structurally impossible".** Binding digests makes a re-run attributable, not impossible. The slice refuses an automatic or edit-free re-run of the last 10 failed pairs (design 16 Q3), and a re-run still follows any visible edit of the suite or the spec.
- **"Per-run eval identity: the eval Job holds its own SVID; the compiler grants it candidate-route admission temporarily".** The slice runs no eval Job and grants no identity. An eval controller in the operator's manager sends each case to the candidate revision's Service, with no credential and not through the gateway (design 16 Q1).
- **The runner slot**: runner images and what they reach, `evalrunner/v1`, DeepEval, Inspect, and cross-runner parity. None is in the slice.
- **Content-addressed datasets and reports, the judge, and the eval budget.** Cases are inline in the EvalSuite, the verdict lives in Agent status, and the slice calls no judge and spends nothing itself. "Fail-closed everywhere" still holds for what the slice has: an empty case list is refused at admission, and every failure to reach a verdict holds the candidate.
- **`tool_correctness`, the eval-principal posture, and the cost baseline.** The slice's one metric is `task_completion`, compared mechanically.

Those statements remain the specification of design 16 beyond its first slice, which is not approved.

**Two costs the human accepted with the decisions, recorded in design 16 §1.1:**

- **Q12.** The eval waits for a recorded `Create` or `Lock`, past its deadline too. It does not wait for an incident: a served Agent with no transaction recorded, under design 03's W1 or beside a foreign traffic policy, is evaluated and promoted.
- **Q13.** The missing-policy wedge is accepted in the slice, with its exits. The design 03 amendment that would remove it goes through design 03's own process, and this amendment does not approve it.

Nothing in this amendment is implemented.
