## MAJOR 1 — `03:781`: the fix for the fourth critique's MINOR 6 replaced a loose sentence with a false absolute, which the deadline table two rows down contradicts

The fourth critique said of "so the gate, the re-point and the attribution all key on one object": "One word ('one route') fixes it." The sentence was instead rewritten to:

> "The gate and the re-point key on the same **route** and on different facts about it — the gate on its generation against the Gateway's report, the re-point on `status.activeRevision` and a recorded digest — and **none of the three can now be satisfied by a route the others would refuse**."

There is a route that satisfies the gate and that the attribution refuses, and the parenthetical **in the same sentence** describes it: an out-of-band edit leaves `<agent>-serving` carrying two `backendRefs`. Once the Gateway reports `Accepted` and `ResolvedRefs` at that route's current generation the gate is satisfied; `singleBackend` returns nothing, so `cardAttributes` refuses. For a missing policy's `Lock` with an uncarded `status.activeRevision` the re-point is skipped and the route is left as found, so nothing collapses it back to one ref.

This is not a hypothetical the design failed to consider — it is a state the design **specifies**, twice, three rows below. `03:798`, the J2 deadline row: "A `401` that could not be attributed is named as such: no card digest is recorded for the revision the route's single `backendRef` names — **or the route does not carry exactly one, which is unattributable** whatever `status.cards[]` holds (A77)". `03:799` says the same for the missing-policy row and specifies the message for it. Those rows are only reachable if a probe ran, and a probe only runs once the gate has passed. So the design states the counter-case to its own absolute and builds a deadline message for it.

"The three" also has no antecedent: the sentence names two.

This is the same trade round 3's MAJOR 3 forced the drafter to unwind one paragraph later, at `03:788`, where the section now says the honest thing — "the gate narrows the window rather than closing it, and leaves two residuals, stated and not closed". Rule 7 is the corpus's name for it.

**Fix.** Keep the fourth critique's one-word version, or state the three checks and what each reads without the absolute: "…so the attribution and `ProbingBefore` agree on which backend they mean. The gate, the re-point and the attribution all read one route object and different facts about it — the gate its generation against the Gateway's report, the re-point `status.activeRevision` and a recorded digest, the attribution its single `backendRef` — so none of them can be reading a route another was not. A route can still satisfy one and be refused by another: two `backendRefs` pass the gate and are unattributable (the deadline table below)."

## MAJOR 2 — `03:776`: the new "liveness, not a false credit" clause claims away the policy half's residual by the inference §3.3.2 forbids, eleven lines before the section states its mirror image as a residual

New this round, closing the fourth critique's MINOR 2:

> "The policy half is not re-run here, before or after A77, so a policy whose ancestor status the gateway controller later retracts — without the policy changing, which `authPolicyIntact` would catch — is not caught by this gate. **That is liveness and not a false credit: an unattached policy produces no `401` to credit**."

"Unattached" here is the **controller's** ancestor status, which is what `policyConverged` reads. §3.3.2 exists to say that status is a control-plane fact and never a statement about the dataplane, and `03:788` — eleven lines later, in the passage round 3 made the drafter write — states the consequence for the route half outright: "**The proxy may lag the controller's report**: a route the controller has accepted at its new generation may still be served on the old one."

The policy half has the same lag in the opposite direction. A proxy can still be enforcing `<agent>-auth` after the controller has retracted its ancestor status without the object changing — the Gateway reconfigured, a listener removed, the policy shadowed. In that window the probe gets `401`, `authPolicyIntact` passes (the spec is unchanged), `cardAttributes` attributes, A75 finds nothing counting, and the `Lock` credits and records `mode: apikey`. The proxy then catches up and the route serves unauthenticated under a recorded `apikey`, and nothing re-runs `policyConverged` on a served Agent. That is a false credit on an open route, not a liveness delay.

**I am disagreeing with the fourth critique here, explicitly.** It reached the "liveness, not a false credit" conclusion in its MINOR 2 and the drafter adopted the wording verbatim. The conclusion holds only if the controller's "unattached" implies the dataplane's, which is the one inference this section refuses everywhere else. I am not asking for a policy-half re-check in the slice — I am asking for the treatment `03:788` gives the route half.

**Fix.** Replace the second sentence: "The residue is the route half's, mirrored: the proxy may still be enforcing a policy the controller has detached, and that `401` would be credited. It is stated, not closed — `authPolicyIntact` catches every change to the policy's *spec* on every pass, and nothing catches a retracted ancestor."

## MAJOR 3 — `AGENTS.md:13` and `CLAUDE.md:11` still assert the attribution property A77 reproduced as broken, and A77 opened neither file

Both files, in the paragraph headed "What remains untrue" that states what is true of the system today, read:

Three things make this the amendment's rather than a follow-up's:

- `CLAUDE.md`'s own opening is the cautionary tale for this exact defect: a false line in this paragraph "was false, and it was the first thing every contributor read".
- The precedent is established both ways. A75, which likewise changed what was true of shipped behaviour, is named in both files. And `AGENTS.md` is routinely edited by docs-only design 03 amendment commits — A58 (`2366851`), A59 (`cd5c0e6`), A60–A67 (`b3720b3`) — so "A77 ships no code" is not a reason to leave it.

**Fix.** One clause in each, beside the A75 sentence: "A promotion during that `Lock` can get the previous revision's `401` credited against the new revision's card digest — measured against `main` `3f37c25`; design 03 A77's route gate closes it and is **not implemented**."
