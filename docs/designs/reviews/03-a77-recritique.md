## Verdict: **FAIL — 0 BLOCKER, 4 MAJOR, 10 MINOR.**

The revision is a large, honest improvement. **BLOCKER 1 is closed and closed well.** The re-point is at `ProbingAfter` only, after the NACK read and after the tuple; "written, converged and enforcing" is gone and is replaced by a statement that says the opposite and says why ("saying otherwise would be the inference §3.3.2 forbids"); §3.3.1 gains the write-order rule with a `What it is not` paragraph that disclaims exactly the absolute the first draft over-stated. I walked every stage order the loop can take — entry through `lockMissingPolicy` at `ApplyingPolicies`, a pass entering at `Converging`, a pass entering at `ProbingAfter`, a `!intact` rewind, `foreignAtOwnName`, a failing `ensureAuthPolicy`, a `createTarget` that does not render, `errRouteGone`, and abandonment (`abandons` is false for every re-creation) — and found no path that moves a backend before the policy step. **MAJOR 2 is closed**: keying the rule on the tuple rather than on a within-pass change is durable across a lost or conflicted `persistStatus`, across a restart, and against a route someone else moved, because the gate reads the live route's generation and the Gateway's reported generation and stores nothing. **MAJOR 1, 3 and 6 are closed** in substance: J2 and K2 are named as the implemented places, the missing-policy `Lock` as the specified one; (f) and (g) can be built and their mutations compile and are killed; the inversion is stated with the conditional that prevents it; ADR-0034 Amendment 6 exists and is in house style.

What is left is smaller than what was fixed, but four of it is load-bearing:

- A77 places the route gate "after the NACK read" and says nothing about A75's Gateway read, which sits between the NACK read and the probe today. On A77's ordering the gate breaks the pass **before** that read, so a Gateway-level hold silently clears for every gated pass — `reportAboveServed` returns early while a transaction is recorded, and the carried seed is withdrawn and not put back on a clean return (MAJOR 1).
- §3.3.3's third `Lock` case — the paragraph the first critique's MAJOR 4 asked for — states the terminal condition as "no digest for `status.activeRevision`", which is half of it. §5, the deadline row and §11 all say "and none for the revision the route names". The body is authoritative, and as written it says an Agent pages for ever in a state where its `Lock` finishes today (MAJOR 2).
- No sub-case of case 18 kills the mutation that drops the digest condition on the re-point — A77's own answer to the first critique's MAJOR 3 — and (b)'s new mutation is not reached by (b), because under the conditional (b) makes no re-point at all (MAJOR 3).
- ADR-0034 Amendment 6 records the placement, the tuple keying, the digest condition and the terminal message as the human's decision and accepted costs of 2026-09-15, while design 03 §11 tags each of them as an answer to a critique finding of 2026-09-16 (MAJOR 4).

## MAJOR 1 — the route gate's placement against A75's Gateway read is unspecified, and on A77's stated order a Gateway-level hold clears on every gated pass

`03:773`–`03:775` (§3.3.3, `ProbingAfter`):

> "**`ProbingAfter`**: three things in this order, then the same anonymous request … 1. **The route gate, in every `Lock`** … It runs after the NACK read, so a standing NACK breaks the pass before it."

The drafter placed the gate against the NACK read and stopped there. In `runLock` the order inside `case StageConverging, StageProbingAfter` is: `authPolicyIntact` → `freshNack` → (at `ProbingAfter`) `gatewayAuthPolicies` with `out.aboveRead = true` → the `out.foreign` check → `probeAgent`. A77's numbered list inserts the gate, the re-point and the gate again "then the same anonymous request", which reads as **before** A75's read. Two consequences, both against approved, implemented behaviour:

1. **A75's hold is not reported on a gated pass.** `reportAboveServed` returns at once while `auth.Transaction != nil`, so nothing else re-derives it. `reconcileGateway` withdraws the carried seed on the way in, and puts it back only on the error path (`err != nil && !out.aboveRead`). A pass that breaks cleanly at the route gate therefore leaves `PolicyApplyIncomplete` unset for a J2 or K2 `Lock` (the reCreate branch keeps `AuthPolicyMissing`, so it is masked there). The Agent's only condition is `GovernanceSkipped=AuthLockPending`, which design 10 never pages on (`10:50`), and `Ready` is not withheld for the hold. So an Agent that A75 makes `Degraded` and pages flaps back to un-held for exactly the passes the gate holds — the window after every promotion, and for as long as the Gateway's route status lags.
2. **Two statements A75 owns go false.** §3.2's A75 subsection says the read runs "on every `ProbingAfter` pass"; §3.2's scale bullet, which A77 itself edited in this revision (`03:353`), still counts "One Gateway `get` and one paged policy list … per `ProbingAfter` pass". Rule 7: a bound the code would no longer keep.

This is not hypothetical ordering pedantry: the gate is unmet precisely when the route just moved, which is the same event that makes a Gateway-level `Allow` rule most interesting.

**Fix.** Say where the gate sits in full: after `authPolicyIntact`, after the NACK read, **after A75's Gateway read and the foreign-policy check**, and immediately before the probe. That keeps every A75 statement true — `held` is already computed before the break, so `PolicyApplyIncomplete=GatewayAuthPolicy` is still raised for a gated pass — and costs the gate nothing, because it guards the *answer*, not the reads. Then state that the re-point runs after the same reads, and correct §3.2's scale bullet if the ordering chosen makes any read conditional.

## MAJOR 2 — §3.3.3's terminal state is stated with half its condition, in the authoritative body

`03:766` (§3.3.3, the third `Lock` case):

> "**This `Lock` still has a state it never finishes** … when `status.cards[]` records no digest for `status.activeRevision`, there is nothing to re-point onto that could attribute, **the route is left as found, and no later pass changes that without a card**. The Agent then holds `GovernanceSkipped=True` and `PolicyApplyIncomplete=True`, both reason `AuthPolicyMissing`, `Ready=False` and phase `Degraded`, and design 10 pages for as long as the Agent exists."

The route being left as found is right. "No later pass changes that" is not: if the route as found names a revision `status.cards[]` **does** record a digest for, `cardAttributes` returns true and the `Lock` reaches `Served` on the next attributable `401`. That is the carded-r1/uncarded-r2 state, and it is the exact state A77's own conditional exists to preserve — `03:1422` says of it "With it, that Agent keeps finishing".

Three places in the same change state it correctly and disagree with the body:

- `03:1317` (§5's row): "no card digest is recorded for the revision the route names **and none for** `status.activeRevision`";
- `03:795` (the deadline row): "because `status.cards[]` records no digest for `status.activeRevision` **either**";
- ADR-0034 Amendment 6, cost 2: "No card digest for the revision the route names **and** none for `status.activeRevision`".

The Status line says "the body above is authoritative", so the wrong one wins. An implementer following it emits the terminal message, with both exits, for an Agent whose `Lock` is one pass from `Served` — rule 8's loud-and-wrong in the other direction, and the residual the first critique's MAJOR 4 asked to be carried correctly.

**Fix.** At `03:766`, make the condition both halves, as the other three places have it: "when `status.cards[]` records no digest for the revision the route names **and none for** `status.activeRevision`". The rest of the paragraph, including the two exits and the rule-8 sentence, is right and needs no change.

## MAJOR 3 — nothing in case 18 kills the mutation that drops the digest condition, and (b)'s mutation is not reached by (b)

`03:1379` (case 18 (b)):

> "**(b) The re-point is not an attribution.** (a)'s build with r2 **uncarded** as well: **the route is left as found** … Mutation: make `cardAttributes` return true whenever the route's single `backendRef` names `status.activeRevision`, ignoring `status.cards[]`."

In (b) the route is left on r1 and `status.activeRevision` is r2. The mutation fires only when the route's single `backendRef` names `status.activeRevision`, which in (b) it never does. The mutation is therefore **not reached**, and (b) passes with it in place. It survives (a) too — there the route does name `status.activeRevision`, and r2 is carded, so the real `cardAttributes` already returns true and the mutation changes nothing. It is not reached in (c) (held at `ApplyingPolicies`), is irrelevant in (e), and (d) is (a)'s build. So the mutation is killed by nothing. That is the first critique's MINOR 7 in a new form, and it is now worse: the sub-case's title no longer matches its content, because under A77's own conditional **(b) makes no re-point at all**.

The larger gap is what (b) was the only candidate to cover. Walk case 18 for the mutation "drop the recorded-digest condition and re-point unconditionally":

- (a): `status.activeRevision` is carded, so the condition is satisfied either way — survives;
- (b): the mutation moves the route to r2, but (b) asserts only that the `Lock` never reaches `Served`, which is still true (r2 is uncarded) — **survives, unless (b) asserts which revision the message names**;
- (c): held at `ApplyingPolicies`, never reaches the re-point — survives;
- (d): (a)'s build, condition satisfied — survives;
- (e): the route is gone — survives;
- (f), (g): J2 and K2, no re-point — survive.

So **A77's answer to the first critique's MAJOR 3 — the conditional that stops the re-point inverting a `Lock` that finishes today — is enforced by no owed test.** That is the corpus's "a test that cannot fail" shape applied to the amendment's own correction.

This also answers the question the drafter flagged as untested. The deadline row (`03:795`) now requires the message to say "**whether the route was re-pointed onto `status.activeRevision` or left as found**" and to name the revision the route's single `backendRef` names. **That needs pinning, and pinning it closes this finding**: it is the one assertion the unconditional-re-point mutation cannot satisfy.

**Fix.** Rebuild (b) around what it now actually exercises — the skipped re-point and the terminal message — and give it the mutation that matters: *re-point unconditionally, ignoring the recorded-digest condition*. (b) must then assert that past the deadline the message names **r1** (the revision the route kept) and says the route was **left as found**; under the mutation it names r2 and says re-pointed, and the case fails. Keep a second mutation on the attribution if the drafter wants `cardAttributes` pinned, but state it so (b) reaches it — e.g. *make `cardAttributes` return true whenever the route carries any `backendRef`*, which (b) does reach and which (a) survives. Rename (b) accordingly; "the re-point is not an attribution" is no longer what it tests, and under the conditional no sub-case can test it, because re-point implies carded implies attributable.

## MINOR findings

1. **`03:785` carries two provenance tags, the second a leftover.** The "Safe because the route was already open" bullet ends `*(Added by A77, and **not implemented**.)* *(Added by A77, and **not implemented** for a missing policy's `Lock`.)*` — the first draft's tag was not removed when the new one was added, and the two say different things. Keep one.
2. **Case 18 (g) says K2 records no `mode`, and it does.** `03:1384`: "which takes the same branch of `runLock` and differs only in its message and **in recording no `mode`**". `lockServed` → `recordServed` → `servedRecord` writes `Mode: apikey` for every credited `Lock`, and §8.1 case 17 asserts exactly that ("reaches `Served` with `status.auth.mode: apikey`"). What K2 lacks is a **recorded mode at entry** — `k2 := status.Auth.Mode == ""` — which is what §3.3.3's K2 bullet means by "no `mode` is recorded". Restate as "differs only in its message and in having no recorded mode when it starts".
3. **The `singleBackend` narrowing is not carried into three older rows.** §3.3.3's deadline table, J2 row (`03:794`): "no card digest is recorded for **a serving revision**". §5's J2 row (`03:1314`) and §5's deleted-`<agent>-auth` row (`03:1317`): "while a card digest is recorded for **a serving revision**". After A77 the rule is the route's single `backendRef`, and a route not carrying exactly one is unattributable whatever `status.cards[]` holds. A66's check found the same shape (a decision not carried into older text) and cost an amendment; carry it now.
4. **"can never move attribution off a carded revision onto an uncarded one" is true only at the write.** `03:775`. A failed drift re-read clears a recorded digest in place (`recordCardAttempt` sets `c.Digest = ""`), so a re-point made while `status.activeRevision` was carded can leave the route on a revision that is uncarded one interval later — and the `Lock` then pages where, with the route still on the carded r1, it would have finished. It ends when that revision's card records again, so it is a delay and not the terminal state, but it is the inversion the conditional is written to exclude. Rule 7: qualify the absolute ("at the write") and name the residual beside it.
5. **A route that keeps moving holds every `Lock` past its deadline.** The gate is a precondition, so each promotion rewinds the transaction, and an Agent promoting faster than the Gateway reports never probes. That is the right trade against a false credit, and the 4-minute deadline pages rather than spins (the 12-step bound in `runLock` catches a single oscillating pass). But A77 states it nowhere. Say it beside "What it costs": the gate converts a false credit into a `Lock` that cannot finish while the route keeps moving, and that is accepted.
6. **The design 16 list misses the two bullets A77's own attribution narrowing breaks.** Under "How the message is computed", design 16 says the exits are named when "no `backendRef` **in any rule** of the route names a revision whose card `status.cards[]` records. **That is `cardAttributes`' own reading**", and under "R's name", "When **several refs** name revisions with no card, the message names each, and it offers the revert exit only when there is exactly one." A77 makes a route not carrying exactly one `backendRef` unattributable, so both rest on a reading that no longer exists. The reachable consequence inside the slice is nil — the slice's serving route carries exactly one `backendRef`, which is also why A77's own justification for the narrowing ("a route momentarily carrying two backends") names a state the slice cannot produce — but the list is the deliverable, and this is the one item the revision itself created.
7. **One of the two "unbuildable" envtest rows is only unbuildable on one reading.** `03:1437` keeps "R's name in the wedge message" on the list. At `bb3a6a1` that row reads "in the wedge state, once C is desired …"; in design 16's **base** wedge row C is a held candidate, so `status.activeRevision` is still R, A77's re-point targets R, the route does not move, and the row still builds. It is unbuildable only if it inherits the preceding "apart" row's state, where C has been promoted. Say which, or drop it and keep the "apart" row, which is unambiguously unbuildable.
8. **§3.2's new scale sentence is imprecise in two ways.** `03:353`: "A `Lock` of a missing policy adds, per `ProbingAfter` pass, one `get` of the route and one list of this Agent's owned routes for the collection that follows the re-point". The `get` of the route already happens every pass, at `currentServingRoute` at the top of `runLock`; what is added is `ensureServingRoute`'s own `Get`. And the list runs only on a pass where both re-point conditions hold, not per `ProbingAfter` pass — `03:775` says "When either fails … nothing is collected".
9. **Why `Create` needs no route gate is stated only in provenance, and only for the attribution.** `03:1426` says "`Create` is unaffected: its route is prepared, and `cardAttributes` has one call site, in `runLock`" — which covers the narrowing, not the gate. The gate's reason is separate and worth one clause in the body: a `Create`'s route is prepared, so no `backendRef` can move under it, and a promotion during a `Create` rewrites only the revision-digest annotation, which `equalRoute` compares but which does not move `metadata.generation`, so `routeConverged` keeps holding. Both halves check out; say them where an implementer reads the gate.
10. **The `ProbingAfter` list reads as the whole stage, and two of its dependencies are not in it.** `03:774`'s numbered list names the gate, the re-point and the gate again, but the re-point's first condition is `<agent>-auth` "read present and equal to the target in this pass" and the gate "runs after the NACK read" — neither the intact read nor the NACK read appears as a step, and `Converging` above it is now one clause. A reader building from the list alone gets neither. Name them as steps 0 and 0a, or say the list is what A77 adds to a stage that already does both.

## The questions, answered

**(1) BLOCKER 1 — is the placement now sound in every stage order?** Yes, with MAJOR 1's ordering left to state. Entry through `lockMissingPolicy` records `ApplyingPolicies`, writes the policy, and reaches `Converging` in the same pass, where `policyConverged` on a just-written policy normally breaks before any re-point; a pass entering at `Converging` or at `ProbingAfter` re-reads `authPolicyIntact` first and a `!intact` rewind returns to `ApplyingPolicies`, so no path reaches step 2 with the policy absent or wrong; `foreignAtOwnName`, a failing `ensureAuthPolicy` and a standing NACK all break earlier; `createTarget` failing returns before anything; `currentServingRoute` nil-or-unpublished displaces to `recreateRoute`; and abandonment cannot interleave, because `abandons` is false for every re-creation. Step 3 works as claimed: `ensureServingRoute` returns the object the `Update` wrote, whose `Generation` is the new one while `Status.Parents` still reports the old, so `routeConverged` fails and the pass breaks before probing. Re-entering the `case` twice in one pass makes the re-point run twice, and `equalRoute` makes the second a no-op. The withdrawn safety claim is genuinely withdrawn, and `03:785`'s replacement says the honest thing.

**(2) MAJOR 1 — can (f) and (g) be built, and are their mutations killed?** Yes to both. (f) is buildable in envtest: `acceptRoute` writes the route's parent status at the route's current generation, so a test that accepts, then promotes a carded r2, gets exactly the state — `ensureServingRoute` at the top of `runLock` moves the `backendRef`, A61 clears `beforeObserved` because `singleBackend(rt)` no longer names `beforeRevision`, `cardAttributes` reads r2's digest off the post-write object, and `creditable` is true today. (g) takes the same branch; `k2 := status.Auth.Mode == ""` changes only the message. (f)'s mutation "gate the route check on `isReCreation`" compiles (`reCreate` is already in scope) and is killed by (f) and (g) while (a)–(e) survive it, which is precisely the argument for owing them. (g)'s mutation "key it on `status.auth.mode != \"\"`" compiles and disables the gate for K2 alone — the missing-policy `Lock` has `mode: apikey` and J2 has `mode: none`, so both keep it. The scope statement is now right: J2 and K2 implemented, the missing-policy `Lock` specified.

**(3) MAJOR 2 — does the precondition hold?** Yes. It survives a lost or conflicted `persistStatus` (the route sits at the new generation and the stored stage stays `ProbingAfter`; the next pass re-derives the gate from the live route and fails it), a restart between the route write and the status write (same state), and a route someone else moved (`currentServingRoute`/`ensureServingRoute` read it and the gate keys on its current generation). It stores nothing, so there is no field to lose — which is the whole improvement over "records the change". I could not construct a livelock: `ensureServingRoute` writes only on drift, so a stable `status.activeRevision` converges; a route that keeps moving is an Agent that keeps promoting, and the deadline pages rather than spins (MINOR 5 asks only that this be said). **It changes no existing passing test's behaviour** as far as I can see: every `Lock` case in `test/envtest/authlock_test.go` calls `acceptRoute` after the route has settled, and `ensureServingRoute` writes only on drift, so the generation the test accepted is the one the gate would compare; the promotion cases (`cardpromo`, `cardstale`) call `acceptRoute` after the promotion and assert the `Lock` does **not** reach `Served`, which the gate only reinforces; K2's case accepts after the first `lockPass`. The e2e runs a real gateway, which reports at the new generation. An implementer should still re-run the suite, because the gate is a new reason for a pass to break.

**(4) MAJOR 3 — is the condition correct and implementable, and is the inverse stated?** Correct and implementable. "Read exactly as the attribution below reads it" is unambiguous and resolves the keying question: `cardAttributes` matches `WorkloadName(agent.Name, c.Revision)` against the ref's name, so the condition is "some `c` in `status.cards[]` with `c.Digest != ""` and `c.Revision == status.activeRevision`" — **not** `hasCardFor`, which keys on `RevisionDigest`. The two agree while revision name and revision digest stay one-to-one, and the spec's phrasing is the safe one; it would be worth naming `hasCardFor` as the function **not** to reach for, since it is the obvious one. The inverse is stated with its cost, at `03:1422` and in ADR-0034 Amendment 6's third cost bullet. Two gaps: the absolute is true only at the write (MINOR 4), and nothing pins the condition (MAJOR 3).

**(5) MAJOR 4 — is the residual accurate and rule-8-clean?** It is in the right places now — §3.3.3's case and §5's row, as text with no "not implemented" wrapper around the state itself — and the rule-8 sentence is correct: after A77 the operator can distinguish terminal from transient, so a "waiting" message would be loud-and-wrong. Both exits are named with their costs, including the unbounded one under a Gateway-level or foreign hold, and the revert's uselessness for a card that never validates. The message itself is free of rule-8 problems. The condition attached to it is not (MAJOR 2).

**(6) MAJOR 5 — is the design 16 list complete and free of over-claims?** Much improved and nearly there. Every added item is real at `bb3a6a1`: the second wedge paragraph, Q12's recommendation, the dependency row, the exemption and its §14 S1 rationale, and "The wedge is design 03's" first sentence; the mis-attribution of "A missing-policy `Lock` does not move the route…" is corrected to "How it composes with what exists", which is where it is. The two narrowings are right and I verified the mechanism: a foreign policy stored at `<agent>-auth` after the `Lock` is recorded holds it at `ApplyingPolicies` through `foreignAtOwnName`, is never taken over (`policyCollision` refuses) and is never deleted, `authHoldsPromotion` holds for I1 alone, so the route stays on R while promotions continue and `collectGarbage` eventually takes R — "Leaving it standing" and "Not exits" survive in that case, exactly as A77 now says.
