## MAJOR 1 — `03:777`: the new NACK case's conclusion is on the wrong side of the 4-minute deadline, and "for all three kinds" is false for one of them

New this round, closing the fifth critique's MINOR 3:

> "Two cases reach it, both shipping today and for all three kinds. … **And a NACK**, whose own rule rewinds the stage on the spot (§3.3): `freshNack` runs before the A75 read, so nothing is carried; while the Event stands `PolicyApplyIncomplete` is re-raised under `AuthLockUnverified`, so only the hold's reason is lost, but **once the Event expires with the tuple still unmet nothing raises it and the Agent reads `Ready`**."

The first two clauses are right, and I verified them: `freshNack` runs immediately after `authPolicyIntact` and before `above = r.gatewayAuthPolicies(...)`, sets `tx.Stage = StageConverging` and breaks, so `out.aboveRead` is false and `storedHold` has nothing at `Converging`; and `runLock`'s tail raises `PolicyApplyIncomplete=AuthLockUnverified` under `if deadlinePassed || out.nack != ""`, so while the Event stands only the reason is lost. The conclusion is the opposite of the code.

- `AuthTransactionDeadline` is **4 minutes** (`authtxn.go:46`). A NACK Event expires at the API server's default of **one hour** — the operator's own message says so, in `authnack.go`'s "wait for the API server to expire it, one hour after it was last seen by default". By expiry, `deadlinePassed` has been true for about fifty-six minutes, so the same tail arm raises `PolicyApplyIncomplete=AuthLockUnverified` and sets `out.withhold`. `Ready` is withheld, not read. Expiry is the one moment at which the design's conclusion cannot hold.
- The window in which the Agent does read `Ready` is the mirror image: the Event goes away **before** the deadline — deleted by hand, or made stale by a policy rewrite whose `policyWrittenAt` passes the Event's `lastSeen` — with the tuple unmet and nothing held. That is a different trigger, and it is the trigger the paragraph's **other** case has: the out-of-band edit rewrites the policy, so `freshNack` finds nothing, and before the deadline the tail sets neither arm.
- "for all three kinds" is false for one. The `reCreate` branch of the tail returns before the `deadlinePassed || out.nack` arm is reached, setting both conditions to `AuthPolicyMissing` on every pass, so a missing policy's `Lock` can never read `Ready` — which this same paragraph says four sentences earlier ("invisibly for a missing policy's, whose `AuthPolicyMissing` masks it"). The scope sentence and the conclusion contradict each other across five lines.
- The same omission sits in the paragraph's load-bearing sentence and in `03:1425`'s copy of it: "The Agent would read `Ready` between the re-point and the Gateway's report" and "an Agent A75 makes `Degraded` read `Ready` for **every pass** between the re-point and the Gateway's report". Under the rewind that is true only until the deadline; after it the deadline arm raises `AuthLockUnverified`. The argument for staying at `ProbingAfter` survives — the reason is still wrong and design 10's clock still restarts, which is what case 18 (h) asserts — but the sentence overstates it.

This is the failure mode the fifth critique recorded against the fourth ("the drafter adopted the wording verbatim"), repeated with the fifth's wording: the finding's suggested sentence was taken as written and not re-derived.

**Fix.** "Two cases reach it, both shipping today. **An out-of-band edit of `<agent>-auth` during the gate's window**: … and before the deadline nothing raises `PolicyApplyIncomplete`, so a J2 or K2 Agent A75 had made `Degraded` reads `Ready`. **And a NACK**, whose own rule rewinds the stage on the spot (§3.3): `freshNack` runs before the A75 read, so nothing is carried, and `PolicyApplyIncomplete` is re-raised under `AuthLockUnverified` — at once while the Event stands, and from the 4-minute deadline onwards whatever becomes of it — so the NACK costs the hold's reason and not the page. A missing policy's `Lock` loses neither: its `AuthPolicyMissing` is raised on every pass before either arm is reached." And qualify the two "reads `Ready`" sentences at `03:777` and `03:1425` with "until the 4-minute deadline".

## MAJOR 2 — `03:1389`, case 18 (h): the mutation label added this round makes the survival claim it was added to fix false

## The questions, answered

**(1) MAJOR 1 (`03:781`).** Closed, true and complete enough. I looked for other shapes where the three disagree and found four: the two-ref route (named); a route with a single `backendRef` to an uncarded revision (MINOR 6 — the everyday one, named in the deadline row and not here); a route with zero refs after an out-of-band strip, which is the same class and covered by "does not carry exactly one"; and the re-point's own generation bump making the gate unmet on the pass that re-points, which the design specifies deliberately at `03:776`. None falsifies the sentence. The identity claim — "all read **one route object**" — holds against the code: the gate, the re-point and the attribution all key on `<agent>-serving`, and `03:788`'s cache residual covers the case where that one object is stale, in which case all three are stale together.

**(2) MAJOR 2 (`03:776`).** Closed, and both halves of the new sentence check out. `authPolicyIntact` compares `compiler.Digest`'s projection — `apiVersion`, `kind`, `metadata.name`/`namespace`, the three provenance labels, and the whole `spec` — and absence; it does not read status. "Nothing catches a retracted ancestor" is true: `policyConverged` is called only from the two `StageConverging` arms, and a served Agent's pass never reaches it. The one qualification worth knowing is that a NACK or a failed intact read rewinds the transaction to `Converging`, where `policyConverged` does run — so a retracted ancestor is caught *incidentally* by something else. That does not weaken the residual, which is about the crediting window at `ProbingAfter`.

**(3) The shared paragraph.** Byte-identical (verified by md5, not by eye), true, in register, and correctly scoped in both directions. Reading the paragraph whole, the addition sits correctly: it qualifies the J2 sentence it is attached to, it precedes the A75 sentence without colliding with it (A75's gate is about which policy could answer; A77's is about which generation the Gateway has reported), and the two later sentences that touch attribution — the re-creation sentence and "An API-key route with `backendRefs` is never created, only updated" — are the missing-policy `Lock` and the prepared route, both of which the clause excludes. Nothing in the paragraph contradicts it. The one thing it asserts that the corpus cannot open is "measured" (MINOR 7).

**(4) The seven minors.** Six closed; the seventh (MINOR 3, the NACK path) is the round's new MAJOR 1. Taking the chain in the code: `freshNack` before the A75 read — true; nothing carried, because `storedHold` requires `tx.Stage == StageProbingAfter` and the rewind persists `Converging` — true; `PolicyApplyIncomplete` re-raised under `AuthLockUnverified` while the Event stands — true, by `if deadlinePassed || out.nack != ""`; nothing raising it once the Event expires — **false**, because the same arm's `deadlinePassed` half has been true since four minutes in and the Event's own default life is an hour.

**(5) Anything new, and rules 7 and 8.** New: both MAJORs and all eight minors. **Rule 7**: the two absolutes the fifth critique found are gone, and the sentences that replaced them are correctly hedged — `03:781` now states its counter-case, `03:776` states its residual, and `03:788`, `03:789` and `03:775`'s "not a promise about later" all still check out against the code. What remains of rule 7's shape is smaller: `03:776`'s "A re-point bumps the route's generation" (MINOR 1), `03:1449`'s "the only sub-case that pins the ordering at all" (MINOR 4), and a "measured" nothing records (MINOR 7). **Rule 8**: MAJOR 1 is rule 8 inverted — the design predicts a silent `Ready` where the code pages under a wrong reason — and MINOR 2 is rule 8's ordinary form, a message that would tell an operator a transient card-fetch failure is terminal and hand them an exit they have already taken. `03:767`'s own rule-8 paragraph and the deadline table's missing-policy row are otherwise right, and the gate's unmet condition names its carrier exactly, which I re-verified against `runLock`'s tail.
